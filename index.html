About me

Hi my name is Casper Lepouttre, I like gaming, building stuff and everything that is plane related.

I want to become a pilot in the Belgian Air Force when I grow up and I am already doing internships with them.

This is the kind of aircraft we worked on during this internship, the F16 AM it's an upgraded f16 A from 1981.

It was built by Sabena and was later upgraded to f16 C standards during the mlu (Mid Life Update programm)

During this internship we worked on the engines and installed bombs (real ones), we also went to look at

the new f35 A installation they were building.

During this intership we also got a small version of bootcamp, we slept in tents and ate military rations

we also got to look at some f16's taking of and we also got to go in the simulation and fly the f16.

We also got the experience of demining an airfield with the cbrn (not real bombs this time)

Then the last day we did a parade where we got a certificate and a patch for our internship.
